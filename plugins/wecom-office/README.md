# WeCom Office

Work with WeCom documents, spreadsheets, calendars, meetings, tasks, email, contacts and Drive files within existing permissions.

## Documents and tables

Create, read and update online documents; search and organize documents and manage access rules. Create online spreadsheets and edit their contents. Maintain smart spreadsheet fields, records, views and charts, and smart document pages.

## Scheduling and collaboration

Look up people and availability, arrange events and meetings, and retrieve meeting notes. Create, assign and follow up on tasks. Send, reply to or forward email and send messages to recent bot chats when explicitly requested.

## Files and resources

Search, upload and download Drive files, work with media resources, and use presentation authoring helpers.

## Account and permissions

Scan the official QR code with WeCom to associate a bot and authorize access. Available operations depend on enterprise settings, bot capabilities, the authorizing person's permissions and access to each resource. Scanning does not expand those permissions. Sending messages or email and editing shared content should follow explicit user instructions.

Use these capabilities to organize project materials, maintain business tables, schedule weekly meetings, track tasks and summarize work.
